## Description

Help Bartholemew construct his dream golf course - in space!

Place Stars and UFOs to ensure customer satisfaction with a 100% success rate on hole-in-ones! But remember, every planet you place counts as a stroke for you! Try to stay under PAR.

Don't forget, there are optional satellites to collect. If you're up for the challenge, try to collect them all and stay within PAR!
